My research focuses on computational methods for bioinformatics, particularly in DNA/RNA sequence generation and modeling and spatial transcriptomics multi-omics. My interests include projects related to machine learning, deep learning, and robotics. I'm also actively finding interns in the summer, please email me if there are any opportunities, and also feel free to reach out to me for cooperation on projects or if you have any questions.

On this website, I present my scientific research topics and things that I find interesting, as well as some reviews or paper reading results, mostly related to bioinformatics. (Also, some memes I liked)
